WheelTempo · macOS · 2026-11-24

Privacy Policy

WheelTempo processes wheel input and settings on your Mac. It sends no usage records to the developer.

Scrolling and system permissions

With your permission, WheelTempo uses the pointer position, wheel movement, button and modifier states, and a window’s ID, owning app and bounds. These let it match app rules, scroll automatically and draw an optional outline. It does not save scroll history, typed text, clipboard contents or screen images. It uses no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Local settings and external destinations

Wheel settings, app rules, reading options, shortcuts and language are stored on your Mac. The shortcut recorder captures only the keys you choose while recording. WheelTempo can ask macOS to launch an installed copy of QingMenu. The full local edition can also store gesture notes, menu paths and selected destinations. Gestures use Accessibility to operate the front window or menu. Files and links open in their associated app. Opening a link connects your browser to that website.

Trials and purchases

To calculate the 30-day trial, WheelTempo saves the times of first use and the latest trial check in the Mac keychain and local preferences. Apple handles Mac App Store purchases and purchase restoration through StoreKit. WheelTempo uses Apple-verified transaction information on your Mac to determine access. It receives no payment card details and does not upload transactions to a developer server. Apple manages its store transaction records under its own policies.

Retention, deletion and permissions

Settings stay on your Mac until you change or clear them. In Smooth Scrolling → Per App, select a rule and click the minus button to remove it. In the full local edition, deleting a gesture also removes its notes and destinations. The original files remain. Pause or quit to stop cross-app processing. Revoke permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security. The developer maintains no server-side user profile.
