Using WheelTempo

How can I make scrolling smoother?

Open Smooth Scrolling and select Global. Enable smoothing. Step size sets the distance per notch, and duration controls how long the motion takes to settle. Try the wheel over the ruler as you adjust.

Can I reverse just the mouse wheel?

Enable reverse scrolling in the global settings. WheelTempo adjusts discrete mouse-wheel events. Your trackpad follows its system setting. Some mouse drivers already convert wheel events to continuous input.

How do I keep the original feel in one app?

In Smooth Scrolling, select Per App, add the app, open More and switch off its rule. You can also use the allowlist to apply your adjustments only to selected apps.

How do I start and stop automatic scrolling?

Enable Auto-Scroll, point at the page and press Control + Option + S. Press the same shortcut again to stop. You can change both the shortcut and the speed in Auto-Scroll.

Where are the settings when the Dock icon is hidden?

Click the mouse in the menu bar and choose Open Settings. To bring the Dock icon back, turn on Show WheelTempo in the Dock in General.

What if scrolling feels unchanged?

In General, check that WheelTempo is enabled and that macOS has granted Accessibility access. Test with a notched mouse on a long page and compare it with the built-in scroll tester. If another mouse utility also smooths scrolling, try pausing its smoothing.

How do the trial and purchases work?

The 30-day trial starts on first use and ends without a charge. An annual subscription renews automatically until cancelled. Lifetime access is a one-time purchase. If you buy lifetime access while subscribed, cancel renewal in your Apple subscription settings. To restore purchases on another Mac, use the Apple Account you used to buy and choose Restore Purchases.
